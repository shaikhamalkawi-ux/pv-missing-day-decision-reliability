PV DecisionLab

Fill missing daily photovoltaic data and review which comparison decisions can be supported by the original measurements.

Free research tool · No account · Your files stay in your browser. Estimates are not measured values.

1. Add your data

Use a full calendar month of daily, comparable, nonnegative yield values (2–14 arrays). First column: date (YYYY-MM-DD). Other columns: arrays. Empty, NA or NaN means missing. A recorded zero is not missing. Export Excel as CSV, or paste its table below.

Normalize first if array capacities differ.

If not confirmed, estimates can be previewed but the observation-only review is held.

2. Choose how to fill the missing values

Peer ratio uses complete measured peers and adjacent-day ratios. It requires exactly one array with a three-day gap. Other methods are exploratory for other patterns. No estimated value is reused to fill another gap. Missing values without adequate support stay blank.

3. Results

Estimated shortlist uses completed estimates. The separate observation-only column uses only measured totals and nonnegative missing yield; it is not an equipment or safety diagnosis.

Research use and limits

Methods are from the 106Amen missing-data research. The estimated shortlist is a point-estimate diagnostic, not certified membership. Observation-only decisions assume the surviving measurements are correct. This page does not establish meter calibration, physical bounds, actual outage recovery or field cost savings. The peer estimator is retrospective and can use the day after the gap.
